04 — Philosophy

Principles I
actually follow.

01
Discipline beats noise
Markets generate infinite opinion. Most of it is irrelevant. A clear process, followed consistently, outperforms reactive brilliance almost every time.
02
Compounding is patient
The mathematics of compounding rewards those who can resist the urgency to act. Time in the market is not a consolation prize — it is the strategy.
03
Simplicity scales
The best investment frameworks are not the most complex. They are the ones simple enough to hold under pressure, when markets make complexity feel like wisdom.
04
Reputation is compounding capital
Every transaction, every piece of advice, every stated view builds or erodes trust. In finance, your reputation is a balance sheet — manage it accordingly.
05
Rationality over emotion
Markets are voting machines in the short run. The edge lies in thinking independently, tolerating discomfort, and being willing to be wrong publicly and correct privately.
06
Read widely, invest deeply
The best investors are students of human nature, history, and second-order effects. The market is just the test. The preparation happens everywhere else.
